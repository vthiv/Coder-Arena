What is Coder's Arena?

Code, Compete, Conquer. Win SEGi Scholarships!

Open to Public

Open to SPM Leavers & Pre-University Students. Participants may be from any institution with basic to advanced programming skills.

Team Format

Limited to the first 10 groups only. Each group consists of 3 persons. Register individually and the organizer will group you into a team.

Open Book Competition

Hardcopy notes, books and reference materials are allowed. Softcopy materials including phones, laptops and tablets are NOT permitted.

03 / Rules

Rules & Guidelines

Read carefully before the competition day.

Materials Allowed

  • Hardcopy notes and references
  • Printed books and textbooks
  • Calculator (non-programmable)
  • No phones, laptops or tablets
  • No softcopy/digital materials

Team Rules

  • Register individually
  • Organizer assigns groups of 3
  • First 10 groups secured
  • No late registrations accepted
  • Each participant can only register once

Competition Conduct

  • Arrive on time (10:00 AM)
  • Abide by organizer's decisions
  • Rely on your own group's effort only
  • No external help from teachers, lecturers or tutors
  • Eligibility: Open to the public

Rewards

  • Top 3 groups win scholarships
  • All participants get e-certificate
  • All participants receive goodies
  • Champion gets 75% SEGi scholarship
  • Cash Prize
